# PAYLOAD seed partition (DownloadService / F3 streaming transfer)

Input: `inputs/seeds/payload/spec/` (base.tla generated by generate.py; 114 actions, one record variable `st` with 97 fields), source pin `c411a201`. Machine data: `payload-partition.json` (same directory). Analysis only; no seed file was modified.

## Result in brief

- Proposed groups: **PAY_RCV** (SJ) 20 actions, **PAY_SRC_SERVE** (CJ) 43, **PAY_SRC_SETTLE** (CJ) 48, environment 3 (ENV_NET `LoseConfirmation`, ENV_CLOCK `AdvanceTime`, ENV_CALLER `WaitForResultTransfers`). Five actor-generic actions (`TransactionEmitProgressEvent`, `TransactionProgressCallbackReturn`, `InvokeCallbackSafely`, `CallbackReturn`, `CallbackException`) have their home in SERVE but contribute settler instances (`t \in Settlers`) to SETTLE.
- Field ownership (proposed cut, field level): 63 private, 21 port, 13 conflict. Of the 13 conflicts, 2 are actor-keyed and can be split per group (`pub`, `cb`), 2 are observer-only (`callbackErrors`, `observedTerminal`), 5 are channel/call/token encodings (`reply`, `confirmWire`, `cancelWire`, `finishReady`, `monitorPC`). That leaves 4 real problems: `progressSeq` and `progressTerminal` (a shared lock), `pullPC` (mixed wire and PC), and `shutdownSourcesPending` (an oracle).
- The candidate cut (receiver-budget pass in SETTLE) gives 26 conflict fields, including the receiver-truth matrix `status`/`provisional`/`pending`/`allDone`, `ops` and the whole progress tracker. That cut splits the `_finalize_receiver` claim race and the budget snapshot/recheck window across groups. **Budget enforcement therefore moves to PAY_SRC_SERVE.** The monitor thread then spans SERVE (budget pass) and SETTLE (classification/retirement), joined by a call/return port.
- Cross-process (SJ<->CJ, clock, network) accesses: 105 at action-instance level. Of these, 60 (a) encode a message or its content, 27 are CJ-private or SJ-private values in the shared `pullPC` slot, 15 (c) are clock reads (CJ only; no SJ action reads time), and 3 (b) are oracles. All three oracle accesses come from `shutdownSourcesPending`.
- Correction to the task premise: `sourceStarted/sourceEntered/sourceRegistered/sourceEnded/sourceFailures/sourceSignal` are **SJ receiver-side** state. `download_object` calls `DownloadService._register_source_download` in the receiver process, and `_handle_source_failure` is the receiver's handler for a SOURCE_FAILURE message sent by the source owner's parent (`download_service.py:1238-1308,2000-2041`, `external_process_backend.py:1285-1325`). `DownloadObjectStart`'s guard on `st.sourceEntered` is SJ-local, not a cross-process read.

## Method

generate.py executed in-memory with file writes stubbed and action() instrumented to capture guards/updates/lets after its implicit guard injection; st.X[..] references parsed with a bracket-balancing scanner; implicit reads of CallbackArgs/FirstProgressEvent/ProgressRank and @ (old value) added; actor-generic actions split per actor class (Pull/Confirm/Cancel/Budget/Inline/Worker) with short-circuited reads/writes dropped for RefFinalizeReceiverCommit and InvokeCallbackSafely. Cross-group reads use the proposed cut. "Process" is SJ for PAY_RCV, CJ for SERVE/SETTLE/caller. The candidate-cut numbers come from the same script with the 9 budget actions and the `Budget` finalizer instances moved to SETTLE. Scratch scripts live in `.tmp/payload-part/`.

(4) `pullPC` multiplexes three things in one slot.

## Ports and coarse-variant obligations

### PAY_RCV (SJ)

- sends: PULL(tx, ref=p[1], receiver=p[2], reqSeq, state=index[p]) at DownloadObjectStart (sync) / DownloadRequestWorkerStart (pipelined); CONFIRM(ref, receiver, status=consumerResult[p], nonce) at ConsumerSendConfirm, only when the terminal REPLY carried a nonce; CANCEL(ref, receiver) at ConsumerConsumeException (always), ConsumerReceiveError (index>0), ConsumerSourceAbortBegin (canCancel); no nonce
- consumes: REPLY(inReplyTo, rc in {DATA,EOF,ERROR,PROCESS_EXCEPTION,MISSING}, nonce?); SOURCE_FAILURE(source_fqcn, ref_ids, reason) from the source owner's parent (SourceFailureNotify); SJ_SHUTDOWN (local DownloadService.shutdown of the SJ process; today driven by CJ Shutdown via shutdownSourcesPending); DOWNLOAD_START(descriptor) from the SJ workflow/FOBS decode (implicit today: all pairs enabled at Init)
- exposes (state read by others): consumer[p] terminal (done/failed/cancelled); consumerResult[p] (materialized success/failed); consumerReturn[p] (returned vs DownloadCancelled); abortNotified[p]
- reads from other groups: none after re-encoding (today: pullPC, reply, replyNonce, shutdownSourcesPending)
- exported port fields (machine): `consumerNonce`, `consumerResult`, `futureStarted`, `index`
- imported port fields (machine): `replyNonce`
- conflict fields touched: `cancelWire`, `confirmWire`, `pullPC`, `reply`, `shutdownSourcesPending`
- coarse variant must reproduce on its ports:
  - Per pair: >=1 DATA PULL then an EOF PULL; the next PULL may be in flight/admitted at the source while the consumer still consumes, so a CANCEL can be sent while a PULL is admitted (needed to keep MC-2 reachable).
  - CONFIRM only after a terminal REPLY that carried a nonce; CONFIRM(success) only after materialization (ConfirmedSuccess spans RCV/SERVE).
  - Failure outcomes: consume exception -> CANCEL, no CONFIRM; download_completed exception -> CONFIRM(failed); ERROR reply -> CONFIRM(failed) without materialization; PROCESS_EXCEPTION/MISSING -> CANCEL iff index>0; source failure / SJ shutdown -> CANCEL iff canCancel, download_failed before exit, typed cancelled vs returned.
  - Silence remains possible: never pull, stop mid-stream, or lose CONFIRM (budgets/timeouts are the source-side backstop).
  - At most one outstanding PULL per pair (seed restriction; real retries/max_retries belong to NVNetwork).

### PAY_SRC_SERVE (CJ)

- sends: REPLY(inReplyTo, rc, nonce iff status[p]="none" at obj_served); FINISH_IF_COMPLETE(t) (in-process call, finishReady) after end_op of an accepted finalization; BUDGET_PASS_DONE (monitorPC := classify); user hooks: downloaded_to_one(receiver,status), downloaded_to_all(), progress_cb(event)
- consumes: PULL; CONFIRM; CANCEL; BUDGET_PASS(monitorNow) from the SETTLE monitor loop; TERMINAL_PROGRESS(ref, state) request from settlement (recommended refactor of TransactionDoneTerminalProgress)
- exposes (state read by others): status[p] (monotone none->final, immutable); ops (admitted operation set); txLast (sliding activity); progressStarted/progressBytes/progressOrder (tracker snapshot for terminal events); acquired (get_acquired_receivers API)
- reads from other groups: live, closed, tableLocked (admission gates); shutdown, tombstoneAt (+ finished receiver-status snapshot) for MISSING/EOF/ERROR replies to late PULLs; refTerminal (terminal override); monitorNow; now
- exported port fields (machine): `ops`, `progressBytes`, `progressOrder`, `progressStarted`, `replyNonce`, `status`, `txLast`
- imported port fields (machine): `closed`, `consumerNonce`, `consumerResult`, `futureStarted`, `index`, `live`, `monitorNow`, `now`, `refTerminal`, `shutdown`, `tableLocked`, `tombstoneAt`
- conflict fields touched: `callbackErrors`, `cancelWire`, `cb`, `confirmWire`, `finishReady`, `monitorPC`, `observedTerminal`, `progressSeq`, `progressTerminal`, `pub`, `pullPC`, `reply`
- coarse variant must reproduce on its ports:
  - status[p]: single atomic commit per pair, first wins, immutable; success only from an accepted CONFIRM(success) matching the pending serve nonce; failed via CONFIRM(failed), CANCEL (acquired receivers, all sibling refs), acquire-timeout (never acquired) or idle budget.
  - Operations admitted before closed may finish after retirement/close: late status commits, late progress, late end_op (drain leak, TIMEOUT-then-complete -> COMPLETED with TIMEOUT cause).
  - Late messages: CONFIRM/CANCEL after retirement are dropped; PULL after retirement -> MISSING, or EOF/ERROR from the finished-ref tombstone within FinishedRefsTTL (not after shutdown).
  - REPLY failure kinds: PROCESS_EXCEPTION (produce raised), ERROR (functional), MISSING; nonce only on terminal EOF/ERROR when status still none.
  - txLast refreshed by pulls and accepted confirms only; finishReady only after an accepted finalization.

## Hardest problems for splitting

1. pullPC multiplexes the SJ request lifecycle, the PULL in flight (with futureStarted) and the CJ handler PC; reply is a single slot. Re-encoding as PULL/REPLY messages plus per-side PCs must keep pipelining (next PULL before consume) and "admitted request survives cancel".
2. Message contents are read by reference at processing time (index, consumerResult, consumerNonce); sound only under one-outstanding-request and frozen-after-send arguments, and nonces are booleans. Ports must carry values.
3. shutdownSourcesPending couples two processes' shutdowns (an artifact of the single-process trace harness). Traces that contain shutdown or source-failure orderings encode this coupling, so per-mix replay of them needs an adapter that emits both shutdown events.
4. Progress tracker (_progress_lock state: progressSeq/progressTerminal/refTerminal) is written by SERVE pull/finalizer actors and by SETTLE TransactionDoneTerminalProgress; MC-2 lives here. Needs a request/ack refactor so the tracker is SERVE-private.
5. Receiver-budget pass must sit with confirm/cancel finalizers (shared _finalize_receiver dedup + activity snapshot/recheck). The candidate cut (budget in SETTLE) yields 26 conflict fields vs 13; the monitor thread then spans SERVE and SETTLE (monitorPC token).
6. Retirement/drain gates (live/closed/tableLocked/ops) are narrow ports but carry the bug-prone timing: admitted ops outlive close, a saved TIMEOUT decision retires a now-complete tx. A coarse SERVE/SETTLE must keep these late interleavings.
7. Six actor-generic actions (progress/callback) need instance-level splitting by actor class; observer counters (callbackErrors, observedTerminal) and the global TypeOK/Symmetry are shared.
8. ConfirmedSuccess (status => consumerResult) and the refinement mapping Abs span RCV and SERVE/SETTLE; they become port-level obligations.
9. HandleDownloadMissing pairs tombstoneAt with LIVE status[p]; the source returns a receiver-status snapshot taken at unlink. The split should export the snapshot from SETTLE (possible seed shortcut, not verified by a trace).
