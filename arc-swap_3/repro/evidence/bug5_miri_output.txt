Interpretation:
  * tests/dynamic_threads.rs spawns reader threads dynamically while a writer
    runs `shared.store(...)` and another thread runs `shared.rcu(...)`. New
    threads cause new debt nodes to be prepended to LIST_HEAD on first load.
  * With LIST_HEAD.load downgraded to Acquire, the writer's `Node::traverse`
    (called from `Debt::pay_all`) is allowed to see a stale list head — one
    that doesn't include a node whose slot was set *before* the writer's
    storage swap.
  * The writer's pay_all finishes without paying that slot. The writer drops
    the old Arc. The reader's deref at dynamic_threads.rs:27 (`**old`) races
    with `Arc::drop_slow` on that same Arc.
  * Miri detects the data race on the Arc allocation — UAF.
  * After restoring the fix (SeqCst), seed=1 passes cleanly. Confirmed —
    this is the canonical "stale snapshot in writer scan" pattern called out
    in the brief.
