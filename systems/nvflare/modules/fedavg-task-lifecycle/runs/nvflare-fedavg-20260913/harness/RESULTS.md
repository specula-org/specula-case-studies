They do not decide upstream severity, constitute a semantic model-checking counterexample, or establish production failure frequency. Existing source behavior is replayed without adding desired-contract guards or weakening the validator.

Evidence limits: controlled schedules, cooperative clients, conventional FULL scalar payloads, supported local runner APIs, explicit allocation/filter/executor fixtures, and a local FOBS delivery/ACK adapter.
