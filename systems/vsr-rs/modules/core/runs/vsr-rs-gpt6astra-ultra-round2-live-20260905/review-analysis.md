## Issues Found

- **Required: complete the baseline state/action handoff.** `modeling-brief.md:89-110` specifies assumptions and one-handler atomicity but leaves the spec author to reconstruct state and transitions. Add a compact table mapping replica view/status/log/commit, acknowledgment and view-change tables, recovery state, client state, timers and message queues to source fields; distinguish volatile, persisted and observational state. Map initialization/recovery, message handlers, idle events and output publication to actions and their atomic boundaries. Keep “no targeted extensions selected” if that remains the decision; no new bug hunt is needed to supply this information.
- **Required within that handoff: make the property observation model explicit.** `modeling-brief.md:118-125` describes committed-prefix agreement “across replicas and time,” at-most-once execution and durable publication without specifying retained history, the identity/incarnation scope, or crash-state treatment. Define the required history variables or temporal predicates and connect them to the actions. These definitions can stay at modeling-brief level; a complete TLA+ specification is unnecessary.
- **Minor: tighten Scenario 6's grouping.** `modeling-brief.md:76-87` combines cached-prefix checks, batch observation, missing reply faults, idealized persistence, a timeout latch and unfinished proofs. Focus the scenario on observation granularity/completeness and place the remaining assumptions/proof-scope items in a separate assurance subsection, retaining all stable IDs and verification routes.

The evidence review found no material discrepancy in the checked bug mechanisms. EX-FSYNC's missing directory-publication guarantee is consistent with the [Linux fsync contract](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/fsync.2.html), and EX-NONCE's freshness concern is consistent with the documented non-monotonic behavior of [Rust SystemTime](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/time/struct.SystemTime.html). Their untested crash/transport consequences should retain the existing qualifications.
