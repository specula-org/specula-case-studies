## Remaining fidelity and environment boundaries

- History is a controlled interface fixture, not a running History persistence service. Its already-started/idempotent/missing-work guards are grounded in `service/history/api/recordworkflowtaskstarted/api.go:68-111`. Lines 180-189 allow speculative starts without immediate persistence; durable-or-recoverable History acceptance remains an external assumption. Actual History lifecycle/stamp transitions and Internal/DataLoss disposal are not certified by these traces.
- `pri_matcher.go:249-284` has a root-validator path that removes eligible backlog work and finishes with `errReprocessTask` without a Worker/History-start request. The current harness parks this loop at entry in non-expiry schedules. Its expiry branch is executed. A complete extension needs an independently observed validator handoff and completion/requeue path; it must not invent a History RPC or treat that workerless removal as an accepted delivery.
- `Crash`, `DiscardCrashedWriter`, `PollerDisconnect`, and `ObsoleteTask` remain unexecuted. A process driver must observe submitted store/History operations independently through process death, restart and transport loss. In-process unload/reacquisition does not establish those outcomes. MC exploration of these actions remains modeled environment coverage.
- Only SQLite V1 is executed. Cassandra empty nonterminal paging, PostgreSQL/MySQL outcomes, unresolved cross-process transactions, V2 fairness and its unlocked replacement/merge candidate remain separate extensions. An empty V2 bootstrap drain does not validate fairness.
- Progress requires stable ownership, available store, eligible pollers, finite interference, and eventual processing/lock acquisition. No unconditional Worker delivery, global ordering, weighted fairness or History outbox theorem is claimed.

The requested supplied `MC.cfg` must complete before Phase 3 convergence and targeted hunting can be reported. Existing smaller Phase 2.5 baselines retain their own scope and cannot substitute for it.
